Ashen Causeway assets

All environment, actor and combat geometry is original game-owned Canvas code
in src/game.zig. No third-party bitmap or sampled audio is used.

The original bell/drone score and seven short effects under src/assets/audio
are generated by tools/generate_audio.py using Python's standard library.
They are 22050 Hz mono PCM16 WAV, with soft attack/release envelopes. The score
is eight seconds and streams through the public Audio WAV music facility.
No outside recording, melody or generative-image service was used.

The UI now uses the embedded Source Sans 3 face documented below.
This provenance record does not choose or change the repository's license.

UI update, 2026-11-10: src/assets/fonts/ui.otf is an unchanged copy of
examples/assets/fonts/SourceSans3-Regular.otf (Source Sans 3 Regular, Adobe).
Its original notice is retained verbatim as assets/SourceSansOFL.txt and is
installed in both native and browser asset directories. The game credits name
the typeface. Body and heading glyphs/layouts are prepared during initialization
through public Peas Font/AssetCollection facilities.
