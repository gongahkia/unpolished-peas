# Transformations

Only three transformed forms remain. Each replaces a platforming or combat rule rather than layering a multiplier over Monkey staff combat.

- **Bird**: normal staff attack becomes a dive. Bird has a tiny collision profile, moves quickly, can flap-jump while airborne, falls slowly, ignores water hazards, and carries its latest horizontal flight momentum briefly back to Monkey.
- **Tiger**: staff and dodge are unavailable. Attack is a high-commitment pounce that strips armor, knocks hard, and breaks cracked walls. It is a route-making and armor-breaking verb, not a faster staff hit.
- **Mantis**: attack becomes a short, mostly stationary counter stance. A successful counter staggers the attacker and exposes a long weak point. This includes Warden’s sweep.

`0` returns to Monkey, restoring conventional staff and dodge rules.
