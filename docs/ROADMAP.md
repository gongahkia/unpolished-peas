# Playtest focus

The current scope is one scrolling platform stage, one Warden, three staff modes, three forms, and Echo. Do not add bosses, campaigns, adjacent maps, forms, art assets, or a second combat ruleset before repeated human tests answer these questions:

- Does jumping while independently aiming feel natural enough for close staff fights?
- Is Short’s deflection readable and worth taking point-blank risks for?
- Does the Medium chain feel fluid on platforms without becoming a safe answer to every range?
- Does charged Long create useful lane control across gaps rather than a safe default?
- Do Bird flight, Tiger wall breaks, and Mantis counters appear as natural route/combat choices?
- Can players deliberately prepare and read an Echo sequence, including jumps, before summoning it?
- Do Warden’s gates teach Long, counter, and Echo use through the stage rather than HUD text?
- Does the camera/minimap preserve spatial clarity during vertical traversal and knockback?
