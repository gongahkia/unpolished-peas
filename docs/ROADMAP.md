# Movement-lab playtest focus

Do not restore combat, transformations, enemies, bosses, loot, or progression until repeated human tests answer these questions:

- Does the run/jump/double-jump/wall/roll system feel precise without becoming automatic?
- Are crouching, roll tunnels, platform drops, dive breaks, ropes, vines, and teleporters individually legible?
- Do crates, rocks, bombs, pressure plates, doors, and the tether produce deliberate physical solutions rather than busywork?
- Do seed variations remain recognizable enough to compare movement changes?
- Does the debug HUD explain a failure without replacing player-facing readability?
